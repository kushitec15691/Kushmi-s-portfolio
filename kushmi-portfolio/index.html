Key challenges I solved
Payment sync timing
Stripe settlements arrive in batches and don't match individual Shopify orders one-to-one. I configured reconciliation to match settlement reports against order totals — eliminating false mismatches in Xero.
Refund & chargeback flows
Refunds in Shopify, reversals in Stripe, credit notes in Xero all needed to move in sync. I mapped the full refund journey so no manual journal entry was required post-refund.
Multi-currency reconciliation
Customers selling internationally faced currency conversion gaps between Stripe (settlement) and Xero (reporting). I configured FX rounding rules to keep books clean across USD, EUR and GBP.
Go-live payment continuity
Switching payment processors while the store stays live is high risk. I built a cutover checklist covering active subscriptions, pending payouts, and webhook re-registration — zero downtime.
Stripe configuration
Webhook setup and testing in sandbox first. Verified all event types (payment_intent.succeeded, charge.refunded, payout.paid) before touching production. Built a 48-hour monitoring checklist for go-live day.
What I carry forward
Payment integrations fail silently more than any other type. The webhook event log is your best friend. "Verify webhook delivery" is now step one of every payment integration go-live checklist — not an afterthought.

Cin7 Core process note
Phase 2 is longest. The data migration template I built over 50 implementations catches ~80% of common issues before go-live. Product structure decisions made here cannot easily be undone.
Odoo process note
Phase 1 is longest. Odoo's flexibility means requirements must be extremely detailed upfront. A vague requirement in discovery becomes an expensive reconfiguration mid-project.
